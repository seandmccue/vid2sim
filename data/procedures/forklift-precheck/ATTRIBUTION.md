# Test footage attribution

`p.mp4` is a trimmed, re-encoded excerpt of "Load and Lift: Guide to Agricultural Lift Truck Safety"
(Oregon OSHA 467A, 2005; sponsor: Oregon Association of Nurserymen), from the Internet Archive's
FedFlix / US Government Films collection, uploaded by Public.Resource.Org with a CC0 1.0 public-domain
dedication:
https://archive.org/details/gov.or.osha.467a

The excerpt covers the pre-operation inspection of a counterbalance forklift. Used to exercise the
pipeline during development and as a public-domain example procedure. The video itself is not committed
(see .gitignore).
