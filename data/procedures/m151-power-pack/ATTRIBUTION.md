# Test footage attribution

`p.mp4` is a 1:44–3:36 excerpt (deinterlaced, transcoded) of "M151 Engine Four Cylinder OHV, Part I", a United States
Department of Defense training film (DoD PIN 29940, 1960s), from the Internet Archive via Public.Resource.Org:
https://archive.org/details/gov.dod.dimoc.29940

As a work of the United States federal government it is in the public domain (the archive marks it CC0 1.0).
Used only to exercise the pipeline on a real industrial engine procedure. Not part of the hackathon demo or
submission; the video itself is not committed.
