# Test footage attribution

`p.mp4` is a 20:16–23:32 excerpt (deinterlaced, transcoded) of "Assembling the Deep Sea Outfit", a United States
Department of Defense training film (DoD PIN 21569, 1963), from the Internet Archive via Public.Resource.Org:
https://archive.org/details/gov.dod.dimoc.21569

As a work of the United States federal government it is in the public domain (the archive marks it CC0 1.0).
Used only to exercise the pipeline on a second, unrelated piece of equipment. Not part of the hackathon demo or
submission; the video itself is not committed.
